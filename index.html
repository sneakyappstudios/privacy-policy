Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use our mobile game application (“App”).

1. Information We Collect

The App does not collect any personal information from users.

Users are not asked to create accounts, enter names, email addresses, phone numbers, or share any personal details inside the App.

The App uses third-party advertising services which may automatically collect limited non-personal information such as:

  • Device information
  • Advertising ID
  • App usage data

This information is used only to display advertisements and improve ad performance.

2. Advertising

The App shows advertisements provided by Google AdMob.

Google AdMob may use cookies or similar technologies to show ads. You can read Google’s privacy policy here: https://policies.google.com/privacy

We do not control the specific ads shown to users. Ads are served by Google AdMob in accordance with Google Play policies.

3. Data Sharing

The App does not collect or store any personal information from users.

Limited non-personal information (such as your Advertising ID, IP address, and device type) is shared with and processed by third-party advertising services (specifically Google AdMob) to serve advertisements, optimize ad performance, and prevent fraud.

4. EEA and UK Users (GDPR / UK GDPR Compliance)

If you are a resident of the European Economic Area (EEA) or the United Kingdom (UK), you are entitled to certain rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the UK GDPR:

  • Consent Requirement: Google AdMob requires explicit, informed user consent to show personalized advertisements and access local device storage (such as cookies/SDK cache). We use a Google-certified Consent Management Platform (CMP) (integrating with the IAB Transparency and Consent Framework) to collect and manage your consent preferences. You can update or withdraw your consent at any time through the in-app settings or your device system settings.
  • Your Rights: You have the right to access, rectify, or erase the limited data processed by AdMob, restrict or object to its processing, and the right to data portability. Since we do not collect or store names or email addresses, any data processing requests must be made using your device's Advertising ID.
  • Data Protection Authority: You have the right to lodge a complaint with your local Data Protection Authority regarding how third-party services process your data.

5. California Users (CCPA / CPRA Compliance)

If you are a resident of California, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA) grant you specific privacy rights:

  • Right to Know / Access: You have the right to request disclosure of the categories and specific pieces of personal information collected about you.
  • Right to Delete: You have the right to request deletion of your personal information collected by the App or third parties.
  • Right to Opt-Out of Sale/Sharing: We do not sell your personal information. However, the sharing of device identifiers (like the Advertising ID) with third-party advertisers for personalized ads may be classified as "sharing" or a "sale" under California law. You can opt-out of personalized ads by managing your in-app advertising preferences or by enabling "Limit Ad Tracking" or "Opt out of Interest-Based Ads" in your device's system privacy settings.
  • Right to Non-Discriminrimination: We will not discriminate against you (e.g., by denying access to the App or degraded service) for exercising any of your CCPA/CPRA rights.

6. Children’s Privacy

This App is not designed for children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the EEA/UK).

The App does not include any in-app features such as account creation, forms, chat, or user input that allow users to submit personal information. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided data through the AdMob SDK, please contact us so we can request deletion from Google AdMob.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable physical, technical, and administrative steps to protect information related to the App. Any data transmitted to and handled by third-party services like Google AdMob is encrypted in transit and managed securely according to their industry-standard privacy protocols.
