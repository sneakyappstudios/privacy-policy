Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information is handled when you play Block Puzzle Pro (including the mini games Fruit Ninja and Tic Tac Toe) ("App"), operated by Sneaky App Studios ("we", "us" or "our"), why it is handled, and the choices you have.

In short: We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location. We only count gameplay and ad events (for example how many games are played) to improve the quality of the game, and we only look at them as totals. We cannot see who you are. We do not sell your data, and we do not give your gameplay statistics to advertisers.

1. Information We Collect

The App does not require an account and never asks you to type in personal details.

When you play, the App and the third-party services it uses (listed in Section 11) may automatically process limited technical information:

  • Device information: device model, operating system version, app version, language and screen size.
  • Approximate location: country or region derived from your IP address (never GPS).
  • Identifiers: a random ID that Google Firebase creates for each installation of the App (it is not your name, email or phone number, and it changes if you reinstall the App) and, where permitted, your device's Advertising ID used by the ad services.
  • Gameplay and ad statistics (see Section 2).
  • Crash reports: technical details about errors and crashes.

Your game progress, scores, settings, power-ups and daily streak are stored only on your device. They are not uploaded to our servers.

2. Gameplay and Ad Statistics We Track

We use Google Firebase Analytics to count events inside the App, such as:

  • Games played: each time a game of Block Puzzle Pro, Fruit Ninja or Tic Tac Toe is started.
  • Rewarded videos: which type of rewarded video was chosen (for example Block Puzzle revive, Fruit Ninja revive, Bomb, Hammer, Swap, Rotate, Gift Box or Streak Recovery), and whether it was requested, shown, watched to the end, closed early or failed to load.
  • Rewards and features: power-ups earned, daily rewards claimed, streak recovery progress, revives used and "Rate Us" button taps.
  • Ad display statistics: whether the in-game native ad was shown, and technical reasons an ad could not be shown.
  • Basic game values attached to these events, such as score, lines cleared or combo.

These statistics do not contain your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, messages or precise location. We only view them as totals and averages in the Firebase dashboard (for example "5,000 revive videos watched today"). We do not look at individual players, and we cannot link the statistics to a real person.

3. Why We Use This Information

We use gameplay and ad statistics only to improve the quality of the App, for example to:

  • see which games and features players enjoy, and improve them;
  • balance difficulty, rewards, power-ups and daily streaks;
  • make sure rewarded videos load reliably, give the correct reward and are not annoying;
  • find and fix bugs, crashes and performance problems.

We do not sell your personal information or your gameplay statistics, and we never will. We do not rent or trade this information, and we do not use it to build profiles about you.

4. Advertising

The App is free and is supported by ads (banner, native and optional rewarded videos) served by Google AdMob and its mediation partners (see Section 11). These ad companies may use your Advertising ID, device information and approximate location to show ads, measure them, limit how often you see the same ad and prevent fraud. Where you have given consent (or where consent is not required), ads may be personalized; otherwise non-personalized ads are shown. We do not control the specific ads shown.

Rewarded videos are always optional. You only watch one when you choose to, in exchange for an in-game reward.

Learn how Google uses information from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell or share your information with anyone for their own purposes. Limited technical information is processed by the service providers listed in Section 11, only to run the App: to serve and measure ads, count how the App is used, report crashes and prevent fraud. Gameplay statistics are used by us for improving the App and are not given to advertisers.

6. Your Consent Choices

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: when you first open the App, a consent message from Google explains the choices and asks for your permission. If you do not give permission, the App does not store analytics or advertising data on your device, and only non-personalized (or limited) ads are shown.
  • US states (including California): where required, a message lets you opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information and of targeted advertising. If you opt out, your data is no longer used for personalized ads; gameplay event counts continue to be used only to improve the App.
  • Change your choice at any time: Settings → More Settings → Consent Settings (shown where these laws apply).
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete it in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

7. EEA and UK Users (GDPR / UK GDPR)

We process analytics and advertising data on the basis of your consent, which you can withdraw at any time as described in Section 6. Crash reports are processed on the basis of our legitimate interest in keeping the App working and secure.

You have the right to withdraw consent at any time (Section 6) and to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. The App has no accounts and we do not know who you are, so we are not able to identify which statistics belong to you. For that reason we cannot look up, send you a copy of, or delete individual data on request (GDPR Article 11). You stay in control using the options in Section 9, and statistics are deleted automatically after the retention period.

8. California Users (CCPA / CPRA)

  • We do not sell your personal information, and we do not sell or share gameplay statistics.
  • Advertising partners may receive device identifiers to show ads. Under California law this may count as "sharing" for cross-context behavioral advertising. You can opt out as described in Section 6.
  • This policy lists the categories of information processed. Because the App has no accounts and we cannot identify which statistics belong to you, we are not able to look up or delete individual records; use the options in Section 9 instead. You can always opt out of sale or sharing (Section 6).
  • We will not discriminate against you for exercising any of these rights.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Analytics event data is kept by Google Firebase for a limited time only (at most 14 months) and then deleted automatically. Game progress stays on your device until you clear the App's data or uninstall it.

Because we cannot tell which statistics are yours, we cannot delete them for you. You can do it yourself:

  • Uninstall the App (or clear its data): this deletes your game progress and the random installation ID, so new statistics can no longer be connected to earlier ones.
  • Reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device settings.
  • Withdraw consent in Settings → More Settings → Consent Settings (where shown).

Statistics that were already collected are deleted automatically by Google Firebase when the retention period ends.

10. Other Information the App Handles

  • Internet time check: to protect daily rewards from clock changes, the App reads the current time from public time services (Cloudflare, worldtimeapi.org, timeapi.io). These services see your IP address as part of any internet request; the App receives only the current time.
  • Local notifications: if you allow them, the App schedules reminders (for example daily rewards) on your device. No personal data is sent to a server. You can turn them off in your device settings.

11. Third-Party Services

  • Google AdMob and Google User Messaging Platform (ads and consent messages)
  • Google Firebase Analytics (gameplay and ad event counts)
  • Google Firebase Crashlytics (crash reporting)
  • Ad mediation partners (serve ads through AdMob): Meta Audience Network, Unity Ads, ironSource (Unity LevelPlay), InMobi, Chartboost, Liftoff (Vungle) and Yandex Mobile Ads

Each of these companies processes data under its own privacy policy. Firebase privacy information: firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

12. Children's Privacy

The App is not designed for children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA/UK). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has used the App and data was collected, contact us and we will help delete it.

13. Data Security and Changes

Data sent to Google Firebase and ad services is encrypted in transit. We take reasonable technical and organizational steps to protect information. We may update this Privacy Policy when the App, its services or the law change; the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date.
